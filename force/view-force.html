Scale

WeiHeng WH-C06 / WH-C100 Bluetooth crane scale. No pairing needed: the app listens for its broadcasts (company ID 0x0100). Turn the scale on, press Connect scale, grant Bluetooth permission. ~10 readings/second.

Rig: anchor one end to something immovable, the other to a strap, bar or handle. For pressing patterns put the scale inside a strap loop that goes under tension when you push.
